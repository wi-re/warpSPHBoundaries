## T2.4 — Q2: conditioning of `W⁵/5` (study only; independent of T2.1–T2.3, do it even if they are blocked)

Background. The shifting tensile term is `T = ∫_solid W⁴ ∇_i W dA'` and `W⁴ ∇_x W = ∇_x (W⁵/5)`, so `T = (1/5) ∇_x ∫_solid W⁵ dA'` = an exact edge integral of the kernel `W⁵`, which is a piecewise polynomial of degree 25 (Wendland C2, `w2`: `W ∝ (1−q)⁴(1+4q)`) or 40 (C4, `w4`: `W ∝ (1−q)⁶(1+6q+35q²/3)`). The question is whether the float64 compile of such a high-degree kernel is accurate. **Read `docs/work/refs/q2_probe.py` first**: it builds `W^k` as an `EdgeKernel` in the truncated-power basis `u = 1 − q`, an exact mpmath reference (monomial expansion at 150 digits through `core.block_grad`), and compares the Warp `edge_channels` g0. Reviewer's results (worst |abs err| / max|ref| over 6 points; do not copy them into your table, reproduce them):
`w2: k=1 4e-15, 2 1.5e-13, 3 6.7e-12, 5 (deg 25) 1.8e-8` · `w4: k=1 2.5e-14, 2 1.1e-11, 3 2.8e-9, 5 (deg 40) 7.1e-4`; numpy `np2d.gradient` on `w4p5`: plain float64 8.4e-4, `stable=(8,6)` 3.2e-11, `stable=(16,8)` 5e-17.
Do:
1. `python/warpSPHBoundaries/q2_conditioning.py` (own code; the reference function may follow the probe's idea but you write it): for `family in (w2, w4)` and `k = 1..5`, on a unit square with support 1 and a point set = the probe's 6 points **plus 200 random points** in `[−0.5, 1.5]²` (seed 5) **plus** the 4 vertices and 4 edge midpoints, compute `g0` of `W^k` by (A) Warp `warpbc.edge_channels` (current plan), (B) `np2d.gradient` float64 plain, (C) `np2d.gradient` with `stable=(8,6)`, (D) `stable=(16,8)`, against the mpmath reference. Report per (family, k, route): `scale = max|ref|`, worst `|err|/scale`, worst absolute error and where (point). Report also: the time of `DevicePlan(kernel, device)` construction and the number of plan terms (`nE`, `nV`) for `w2p5` and `w4p5`, and the mpmath reference time.
2. Classification, fixed here with its reason (the shifting tensile term is ~0.14 against a net first-row shift of ~0.03, `HANDOFF.md` A6; an error of 1e-5 of the term's scale is then < 0.05 % of the net): `worst |err|/scale ≤ 1e-8` GOOD, `≤ 1e-5` ACCEPTABLE, `> 1e-5` NOT USABLE. State the class of each (family, route) at `k = 5`.
