* WORK-004 is accepted (`docs/work/logs/REVIEW-004.md`): `cfg.coverExact`, `cfg.coneExact`, `cfg.tensileExact` (C2 and C4) exist, default off. This package adds the **fourth and last wall quadrature**, the wall part of the artificial viscosity (`rhs`, the block `if cfg.viscosity and cfg.wallViscosity and samples is not None`), as `cfg.viscosityExact` (default off).
* **What the current wall term is.** Free-slip mirror, one ghost sample per solid point with the particle's own velocity mirrored in the wall normal `n` (`n = G/|G|`, the unit vector of the wall gradient `G = ∇λ`, pointing **into** the wall; `u_n = (v − v_wall)·n`, positive = moving into the wall): ghost minus own velocity `= −2 u_n n`. In the pairwise Monaghan form of the solver, `acc_i = (α c0 H/ξ) Σ_j V_j (v_ij·x_ij)/r² ∇_iW_ij / ρ̄`, this gives `acc = fac · wallMass · 2 u_n/ρ · (M₂ n)` with `fac = α c0 H/ξ` and `M₂ = ∫_solid W′(r)/r ŷ⊗ŷ dA` (computed by the polar quadrature, `W′ < 0`, so the term damps `u_n`).
* **What the exact wall term is (user decision, HANDOFF Part A, row Q1: "an exact boundary needs the naive Laplacian, not the pairwise form").** The same mirror, the naive Laplacian `ν ∇²v ≈ ν Σ_j V_j (v_j − v_i) ∇²W_ij`: `acc = −2 ν_eff · wallMass · u_n/ρ · Δλ · n`, `Δλ = ∫_solid ∇²W dA′` (`1/length²`, **positive** for a particle on the fluid side of a wall, zero inside a body whose support is fully inside, zero beyond `H`). Only the **normal** component is damped, exactly as in the present term (the boundary condition is not changed: free slip). A constant mirror normal `n_i` per particle (as now) means only the scalar `Δλ` is needed; the first moments `∫ y_a ∇²W` (HANDOFF) would be needed only for a position-dependent mirror field and are **out of scope**.
* **`ν_eff = fac/8 = α c0 H/(8 ξ)` — derived, not calibrated.** For a smooth field `v`, the fluid–fluid pairwise term of the solver equals `(fac/8)(∇²v + 2∇(∇·v))` in 2D (expand `v_j` to second order; the only surviving moment is `∫ r W′ dA = −d = −2` and the isotropic fourth-moment tensor); this is `fac/(2(d+2))` and it equals the `ν` of the solver's own time-step rule (`dtv`: `nu = α c0 H/(2·4)`). Reviewer check (plain quadrature): `∫ r W′ dA = −2.0000` (C2) / `−1.99999999999999` (C4), and for `v = (y², 0)` (`∇²v = (2, 0)`, `∇·v = 0`) the pairwise acceleration is `+0.25 fac` = `(fac/8) · 2` (C2 and C4). The wall term uses the **same** `ν_eff`, so wall and bulk viscosity are one operator.
* **The two wall terms are different operators near the wall — measured, state it in the docs, do not "fix" it.** Flat wall (solid `y < 0`), particle at `(0, z)`, `H = 1`: the pairwise coefficient is `A(z) = ∫_solid W′/r (ŷ·n)² dA = ∫_z^H W′(r) (Θ + sinΘ cosΘ) dr`, `Θ = arccos(z/r)` (negative), the Laplacian one `B(z) = Δλ(z)`; `acc_pair = fac · 2 u_n · A · n`, `acc_lap = −(fac/8) · 2 u_n · B · n`.
