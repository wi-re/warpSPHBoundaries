\section{Analytic tetrahedra and triangular surfaces}\label{sec:threed}

\begin{theorem}[Regular face identity]\label{thm:face}
Let $T$ be a bounded oriented polyhedron, $g$ a bounded radial profile with
support $R$, $M_3(r)=\int_0^r t^2g(t)\,\dd t$, and
$z_f=\nn_f\cdot(p_f-\xx)$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:face-regular}
 \int_Tg\,\dd V=\sum_{f\in\partial T}z_f\int_f\frac{M_3(r)}{r^3}\,\dd A.
\end{equation}
This form includes queries on faces, edges and vertices, without an indicator
or a solid-angle boundary convention.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
With $F=M_3/r^3$, $\nabla_y\!\cdot(\yy F)=3F+rF'=g$ for $r>0$.
The small-sphere flux vanishes as $M_3(\varepsilon)=O(\varepsilon^3)$.
The field is continuous at the support boundary. Apply the divergence theorem.
A face with $z_f=0$ has exactly zero flux in this regular form. [M]
\end{proof}
For $g=r^n\ind[r<R]$, $n\ge0$, the radial flux coefficient is
\[
 F(r)=\frac1{n+3}\begin{cases}r^n,&r<R,\\R^{n+3}r^{-3},&r\ge R.\end{cases}
\]
Unlike a compact flux, it visits all faces, including faces outside support.
Away from $\partial T$, subtracting the fundamental field gives the earlier
compact identity
\[
 \int_Tg=4\pi M_3(R)\ind[\xx\in T]
 +\sum_f z_f\int_{f\cap B_R}\frac{M_3(r)-M_3(R)}{r^3}\,\dd A.
\]
On the boundary its indicator must instead be interpreted through the local
solid angle; the implementation uses \cref{eq:face-regular}.

\subsection{Face-to-edge evaluation}
Let $a=|z_f|$, $\bm q=\yy-z_f\nn_f$, $\rho=|\bm q|$, so $r^2=a^2+\rho^2$.
For a radial face profile $v(r)$ define
\[
 H(r)=\int_a^r t v(t)\,\dd t,\qquad B(\rho)=H(r)/\rho^2.
\]
Then $\operatorname{div}_f(\bm q B)=v(r)$. On each edge write
$\bm q=w\bm\mu+s\tv$, where $\bm\mu=\tv\times\nn_f$ is the outward
in-plane co-normal. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:face-edge-regular}
 \int_f v(r)\,\dd A=\sum_e w_e\int_{s_0}^{s_1}
                  \frac{H(r(s))}{s^2+w_e^2}\,\dd s.
\end{equation}
For $v=r^n\ind[r<R]$, $a<R$, $n\ne-2$,
$H=(\min(r,R)^{n+2}-a^{n+2})/(n+2)$; for $a\ge R$ the integral is zero.
Split the edge at $s=\pm\sqrt{R^2-a^2-w^2}$ when applicable.
Outside the disk $H$ is constant, not zero: these exterior straight-edge
pieces account for the circular boundary, even for a disk contained in the face.

With $b=|w|>0$, $c^2=w^2+a^2$ and $r^2=s^2+c^2$, let
$K_m=\int r^m/(s^2+w^2)\,\dd s$. Elementary bases and a recurrence are
\begin{align*}
 K_0&=b^{-1}\arctan(s/b),&
 K_{-1}&=(ab)^{-1}\arctan(as/(br))\quad(a>0),\\
 K_{-1}|_{a=0}&=s/(b^2r),&
 K_1&=\asinh(s/c)+a^2K_{-1},\\
 K_m&=I_{m-2}+a^2K_{m-2}\quad(m\ge2).
\end{align*}
These identities are checked by symbolic differentiation [M]. An exactly
zero $w$ has zero regular planar flux and is skipped before division.

Writing $S_f(n,R)=\int_f r^n\ind[r<R]\,\dd A$, a complete volume block is
\[
 V_{n,R}=\frac1{n+3}\sum_{f:z_f\ne0}z_f\bigl[S_f(n,R)
 +R^{n+3}\{S_f(-3,\infty)-S_f(-3,R)\}\bigr].
\]
The auxiliary $r^{-3}$ profile is evaluated only for $a>0$.
For $n=0$, $V_{0,R}$ is the sphere--tetrahedron overlap volume, enabling a
comparison with the independent decomposition of Strobl et al.~\cite{strobl2016}.
The associated face areas also compare with their implementation.

\subsection{Moments, derivatives and FEM}
Let $M_\alpha[f]=\int_T\yy^\alpha f\,\dd V$ and
$S_{f,\alpha}[f]=\int_f\yy^\alpha f\,\dd A$.
For $\alpha=\beta+\ee_i$, the same compact potential as in two dimensions gives
\[
 M_{\beta+\ee_i}[f]=\sum_f n_{f,i}S_{f,\beta}[\Phi[f]]
                  -\beta_i M_{\beta-\ee_i}[\Phi[f]].
\]
Expand the face polynomial in $\yy=z_f\nn_f+u\ee_1+v\ee_2$ and apply the same
recursion to $(u,v)$. Boundary polynomials times radial powers use the existing
$S_{j,m}$ primitives, and scalar terminals use \cref{eq:face-edge-regular}.
For $g_{\alpha,j}=\int_T\yy^\alpha\partial_{x_j}W\,\dd V$,
\[
 \partial_{x_j}M_\alpha=-\sum_f n_{f,j}S_{f,\alpha}[W],\qquad
 g_{\alpha,j}=\partial_{x_j}M_\alpha+\alpha_jM_{\alpha-\ee_j}.
\]
Thus the complete analogue of the two-dimensional moment contract has 22
stored entries: $M_0$ (1), $M_1$ (3), symmetric $M_2$ (6), $g_0$ (3),
and the full $g_1$ matrix (9). For a radial scalar kernel $g_1$ is symmetric,
so these 22 stored entries have 19 independent components. Under whole-support
integration $g_1=I$.
With $L=W'/r$, Green's identity and integration by parts give
\begin{align*}
 \int_T\yy^\alpha\Lap W
 &=\sum_fz_f S_{f,\alpha}[L]
 -\sum_{f,j}\alpha_j n_{f,j}S_{f,\alpha-\ee_j}[W]
 +\sum_j\alpha_j(\alpha_j-1)M_{\alpha-2\ee_j}[W],\\
 \int_T\yy^\alpha\partial_{x_i x_j}W
 &=\sum_fn_{f,i}S_{f,\alpha+\ee_j}[L]+\alpha_i g_{\alpha-\ee_i,j}.
\end{align*}
On a standalone triangle the full ambient kernel gradient is instead
$\int_f\yy^\alpha\partial_{x_j}W=-S_{f,\alpha+\ee_j}[L]$, including its
normal component.

For $p>0$, the barycentric lattice basis on a tetrahedron is
\[
 N_b(\lambda)=\prod_{i=0}^{3}\frac1{b_i!}
             \prod_{k=0}^{b_i-1}(p\lambda_i-k),\qquad \sum_i b_i=p.
\]
Exact expansion about the query and contraction with $M_\alpha$ or
$g_{\alpha,j}$ gives P0--P3 weights (1, 4, 10, 20 nodes).
The same construction with three barycentric coordinates gives surface FEM
weights (1, 3, 6, 10 nodes), contracted with area moments.
Boundary traces alone do not define an arbitrary interior field.

On a conforming tetrahedral mesh with a common polynomial integrand,
interior faces cancel pairwise in all these expressions. The resulting
boundary-only evaluation uses a watertight oriented triangle mesh; for
piecewise fields with unequal traces, the weighted face jumps must remain.

\paragraph{Verification and scope.}
\path{maple/22_mesh3d.mpl} verifies the local identities, compact moment
recursion through degree four, and P1--P3 partition/Kronecker values [M].
\path{tests/mesh3d/} checks exact polynomial moments, support clipping,
face/edge/vertex queries, planar limits, independent simplex quadrature,
query derivatives, dilation/rotation, interior-face cancellation, cavities,
and inscribed sphere-mesh convergence for the Wendland kernels [N].
The optional authors' \texttt{overlap} implementation supplies an independent
constant-kernel comparison; difficult cases additionally use positive
clipped-simplex quadrature. Regenerable plane slices and comparison records
are produced by \path{scripts/studies/mesh3d_slices.py} and
\path{scripts/studies/mesh3d_overlap.py}.
The implementation is an arbitrary-precision reference. Stable float32/64
compilation, compact-support face pruning, shape derivatives and GPU/scene
integration remain open; these finite checks do not establish production
accuracy or throughput. \status{V reference; P device}
