What is being integrated?

Occupancy λ = ∫ Wh dV. Solid, porous and free occupancy partition geometric regions and sum to one. Porous occupancy measures the bulk insert region, not solid fraction or porosity. The free complement is outside that region; pore fluid is not separately modelled. Volume derivatives differentiate the kernel; translated moments and material fields have their own complete derivatives.

Compression diagnostic C = ∫ [ufluid(q) − uskeleton(s)] · ∇qWh dV. This is a supplied boundary continuity contribution, with dimensions of inverse time. It is not a pressure residual or a complete porous-density formulation.

Permeable response drag = −∫ D(s)[u(s) − us(s)]Wh dV; reaction = −drag; dissipation = ∫ vᵀDv Wh dV. Drag, reaction and dissipation are displayed per unit fluid mass. Local mobility is [I + dt ∫ DWh dV]−1. Its normal response uses the supplied relative velocity, without a pressure solve. This porous response does not impose the impermeable wall condition. Circle/fibre presets have no single planar normal, so that diagnostic is unavailable. D has units of inverse time; if starting from permeability, the simplified constant-density model is D = νK−1.

Higher degrees use the positive scalar profile [1 + 0.25 ξ(s)]p multiplying the resistance tensor, where ξ traverses the wall from zero to one. Circle/fibre presets use impermeable chord geometry, whose refinement changes geometry. 2D fibres measure transverse response; longitudinal flow needs an axial formulation or 3D. An effective drag is never added to these resolved fibres.

Porous-flow reference: Böttcher et al. (2025), Implicit Incompressible Porous Flow using SPH, v2. Pressure and porosity integration belong to the later solver handoff.
